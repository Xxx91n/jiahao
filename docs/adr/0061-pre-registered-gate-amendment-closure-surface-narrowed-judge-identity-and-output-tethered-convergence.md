## Decision

### D-A — Pre-registered gate revision is a procedure that produces the number, and the procedure and its first application land in one ADR

The tarball-cap amendment (and any future pre-registered gate revision) follows change-management closure: the amendment ADR states the policy section *before* the value section (text order is time order); the new value is derived from in-record evidence (the measured trend 205,741 -> 206,288 -> 206,501 and a composition analysis), not from the next preferred outcome; the amendment explicitly declares it is not a retroactive application of ADR-0039 D3's formula and cites the withdrawn falsified amendment (5567bd8); it carries an independent second_reviewer sign-off (ADR-0047 criteria-change path); and it registers a review_at date in the deferred-registry tide. ADR-0039 D3's recompute trigger is amended from a one-shot narrowing-round anchor (M=140,778, which never fired) to a periodic trend anchor. The measurement protocol (command, size metric, npm version) is pinned before any new value is decided; 258,127 was a forecast, not a measurement. Data-first amendment without procedure is rejected (the withdrawn amendment's structural twin; Barr Labs / OOS / SRE gaming precedents). Procedure-first this round and value next round is a fallback only, with an explicit deadline. Removing CONTEXT.md from the package to pass the gate is rejected (violates ADR-0039 D1 and narrows the tested surface, contradicting coherence-tier discipline).

### D-B — Judge identity stays content-hash; the hash surface narrows to judge-behavioral text

Content addressing remains the identity model (prompt-registry industry consensus; ISO/IEC 17025 §6.4.13 software-version identity). The defect is the dual-purpose single file: hashing all of `src/SKILL.md` drags generator/product wording into the judge identity axis. The identity digest covers the judge-behavioral text surface (the verifier rules region); product-surface edits no longer cascade into identity re-pinning, while any change to judge rule text still triggers the full re-verification chain. This change is a criteria-change and follows DI-A's procedure discipline (same-ADR closure, second_reviewer, review_at). A "semantic digest" of the rules is rejected (JudgeSense: semantically equivalent rephrasings flip judge outcomes 8.5-61.3%; lm-eval-harness: whitespace alone swings scores — abstraction anchors under-detect). Declaring the payload a non-identity input while the mechanism hashes the file is rejected (statement contradicts mechanism; ADR-0040 honest-unverifiable). If a frozen copy is ever used, the judge must actually read that copy at runtime.

### D-C — Instrument defects are tagged per declaration, dispositions are append-only, and the conditional window gets a look-back review

Three defects, three dispositions, no blanket dormancy. (1) The UNRESOLVED sub-declarations (model checkpoint / model identity) are tagged out-of-service at declaration granularity with a recorded re-verification path and a deadline — never open-ended (ISO/IEC 17025 §6.4.9 "until verified"). (2) The seq-3 sign-off gap is closed by an *appended* disposition record (re-record under P-A1, or a disposition with an impact assessment), signed by a second_reviewer distinct from the original signer, with a deadline; in-place rewriting is forbidden (ADR-0050 append-only). (3) sample_size = 0 introduces no new state: n < min_n already yields `indeterminate` (ADR-0060 D-B); the conditional certification explicitly states its conclusion rests on indeterminate conformity. Additionally, the conditional window opened at seq-3 (2026-09-12) receives a §7.10-style look-back impact review of prior results, recorded in writing even if the finding is "no impact". Dormancy of the sign-off requirement itself is rejected (the only control that can repair the defect); continued certification over an unresolvable identity declaration is rejected (zero credit by default).

### D-D — The governance convergence criterion is product-metric movement; the registry tide remains the cadence

ADR-0059 D-C is amended: the review_at tide stays as review *cadence*, but the convergence *criterion* becomes movement of the registered product metric (bench recall, currently 34.7%). Every round must register which registered product metric it moved; "answer" does not mean "must move" — waiver rounds (research rounds, governance-repair rounds) register their exemption explicitly. A zero-movement judgement respects ADR-0060 power discipline (no zero-movement count on a statistically insufficient sample). N consecutive zero-movement rounds trigger a governance-diet agenda; exemption rounds do not count toward N, but 3 consecutive exemption rounds trigger the same alert (balancing "exemption softens the criterion" against "repair rounds misfire the alarm"). Hard quotas (ADR counts or doc line counts in gates.json) are rejected (red-lamp desensitization precedent, tarball cap standing red, governance cost outweighs benefit at low threat). The tide alone is rejected as the criterion (sunset-effectiveness research: a clause without credible termination hazard, review capacity, and agenda time is decorative).

### D-E — Governance artifacts are anchored in layers: authoritative copies in git, machine-generated digests witnessed externally

Audit reports and decision ledgers get authoritative copies in git-tracked storage that stays outside the tarball whitelist (the docs/adr precedent); process evidence (closure logs, raw outputs) stays under `.scratch/`. Digest links in the reference chain (ADR -> ledger -> digest) are machine-generated and machine-verified (regen-and-diff), not hand-copied. ADR-0059's ledger pointer to a gitignored path is relocated (the literal no-anchor defect). Defer-0024 (external witness) receives a minimal-unlock obligation: anchoring is inherently external — even Rekor requires an external monitor, and git alone is not tamper-proof. A hash-only anchor is rejected (cannot re-create the original record; SEC 17a-4 audit-trail alternative requires re-creation); a declarative downgrade ("the ADR paraphrase is the fact source") is rejected (hand transcription drift is ADR-0043's own original incident). Implementation of this decision is registered as a governance-repair round (exemption class) under D-D.

### D-F — Round sequencing: measurement unblocking first, then D-B, then dual track

Two items are measurement defects, not deferrable debt: the persistently red main CI (JIAHAO_CORPUS_DIR capability probe misjudgement violates ADR-0040's exit-2 honest-unverifiable semantics) and the missing pack-cap assertion in gate:all (the tarball cap is guarded only by jest). They are fixed in an at-most-one-round unblocking step (ADR-0027 same-commit discipline). D-B's surface narrowing then lands before any product round, since product rounds edit `src/SKILL.md` and would otherwise trigger identity re-verification per edit. After that, the product round (TF-IDF-family lightweight discriminative features, registered as a research round under the ADR-0059 waiver and D-D) and the remaining governance tails (BL-1, BL-2, defer-0028..0031 tide check-in) proceed on separate GitButler branches with separate agents; the human keeps WIP = 1. Pure "product first" is rejected (metric claims on a broken measurement surface invite Goodhart disputes); pure "governance first" is rejected (consecutive governance rounds confirm the governance-theater charge).
