Repair belongs to the owner: (a) retroactive gtd/carve_out backfill + Disclosed Repair on the zero-R2 claims (doc-surface, t14/t17 precedent), and/or (b) a fix/implementation round amending the taxonomy (README-zh-CN.md -> R3; possibly a standing ci.yml suite-parity exemption) plus the owed anchors admission (below).
